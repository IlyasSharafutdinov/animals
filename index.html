01 · Sea Turtles

Rescued from nets and currents

Sea turtles wash up entangled in discarded fishing nets or injured by boat strikes, unable to dive or feed. Our team frees them, treats their wounds, and works through weeks of rehabilitation before they're strong enough to return to open water.

02 · Dogs

From the street to a loving home

Many of the dogs we take in have spent their whole lives on the street, wary of people and traffic. We give them medical care, patient socialization, and time to learn to trust again.

03 · Penguins

From oil spills back to the colony

Oil spills leave penguins unable to stay waterproof or warm. We wash and treat affected birds, monitor colony health, and return each one home once they're ready.
